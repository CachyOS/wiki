## Usecases:
- **3D acceleration**

In operating systems that make use of `OpenGL`, such as **Linux**, **BSD**'s and **Android**, you can 
achieve much better performance than software paravirtualization by translating calls from the VM 
directly to your GPU and back without having to pass it to a VM.

## Using EGL Headless in VMM:
In **VMM**, go ahead and open up one of your eligible VM's (as described above and using the **User Session** mode).

<Steps>

1. Follow the picture along to add EGL Headless: <ImageComponent imgsrc={import('~/assets/images/vmmegl.png')} />

2. In `Video Virtio` tick on `3D Acceleration`.

</Steps>
