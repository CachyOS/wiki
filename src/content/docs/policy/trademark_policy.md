CachyOS Trademark Policy

## 1. Introduction

CachyOS is free and open-source software, and we want people to talk about it, write about it, and recommend it. At the same time, the CachyOS name and logo tell users where a piece of software comes from and who stands behind it.
This policy explains how the CachyOS trademarks may and may not be used, so that users are not misled about what is and what is not an official CachyOS product.

In short: you may always refer to CachyOS by its name, but you may not use the CachyOS brand for your own software, products, services, or merchandise without written permission.

## 2. The Trademarks

"CachyOS" is a registered European Union trade mark (EUTM No. 019349229), owned by Peter Jung on behalf of the CachyOS project.

This policy covers the following, collectively referred to as the "Trademarks":

- The word mark **CACHYOS**, in any spelling or capitalization (for example "CachyOS", "Cachy OS", "cachyos").
- The CachyOS logo and its variations, as well as other official CachyOS artwork and branding.
- Any name, logo, or domain that is confusingly similar to the above, including names that begin with or contain "Cachy" in connection with software or computing.

The CachyOS logo and artwork are additionally protected by copyright.

## 3. Use That Does Not Require Permission

You do not need permission to use the name "CachyOS" in plain text to truthfully refer to the CachyOS project and its software. This includes:

- News articles, reviews, blog posts, videos, tutorials, benchmarks, and other commentary or criticism about CachyOS.
- Community discussion, support, and advocacy for CachyOS.
- Truthful statements of fact about your own project, such as "based on CachyOS", "compatible with CachyOS", or "tested on CachyOS", as long as the Trademarks are not part of your project's name, logo, or branding.
- Showing the unmodified CachyOS logo next to such commentary to identify CachyOS, in accordance with the [Logo Usage](#6-logo-usage) section.

In all of these cases, the use must:

- Refer to the genuine, unmodified CachyOS project or its software.
- Not suggest that you, your project, your product, or your service is official, or is sponsored, endorsed, or approved by CachyOS.
- Not be more prominent than your own name or branding.
- Not disparage or mislead about CachyOS. Honest criticism and clearly recognizable parody are not affected by this.

### 3.1. Community Packages

Community-maintained packages of software published by CachyOS may keep the name of that upstream software (for example `linux-cachyos`) as the name of the package, and of the build recipe or package repository that carries it, without permission, as long as:

- The package is built from the sources published by CachyOS, with only the changes needed to build and package it for the target distribution.
- The package is clearly described as an unofficial, community-maintained package that is not provided or supported by CachyOS.
- The Trademarks are used only to identify the packaged software, and not as the name or branding of a distribution, product, or service.

This exemption covers the package name only. It does not permit redistributing the CachyOS repository or packages obtained from it, which remains governed by the [Repository Usage Policy](/policy/repository_policy).

## 4. Prohibited Use

Any use of the Trademarks that is not listed in the section above requires prior written permission. Without such permission, the following is **STRICTLY PROHIBITED**:

### 4.1. Software and Distributions

- Using the Trademarks in the name, logo, or branding of any software, operating system, distribution, fork, spin, remix, ISO, installer, container image, virtual machine image, kernel build, repository, or application that is not published by the CachyOS team, except as described in the [Community Packages](#31-community-packages) section.
- Distributing a modified version of CachyOS, or of software published by CachyOS, under the CachyOS name or with CachyOS branding.
- Shipping the CachyOS name, logo, or artwork as the branding of another distribution or product, for example in the boot screen, installer, desktop theme, `os-release`, or system information.
- Offering hardware, hosting, support, or other services under the CachyOS name, or presenting them as "official", "certified", or "CachyOS approved".

### 4.2. Merchandise

- Producing, selling, or giving away any merchandise that carries the Trademarks. This includes, but is not limited to, clothing, stickers, mugs, posters, keycaps, mousepads, cases, and any other physical or digital goods.
- Offering designs that contain the Trademarks on print-on-demand platforms or marketplaces.

### 4.3. Names, Domains, and Accounts

- Using the Trademarks, or anything confusingly similar, in a company, organization, product, or project name.
- Registering or using domain names, social media accounts, channels, or usernames that contain the Trademarks in a way that could be mistaken for an official CachyOS presence.
- Combining the Trademarks with other words, marks, or logos to create a new name or logo.
- Using the Trademarks in page titles, metatags, or advertising keywords mainly to attract traffic or influence search results.

### 4.4. Affiliation

- Any use that suggests an affiliation with, or an endorsement, sponsorship, or approval by CachyOS that does not exist.

## 5. Open-Source Licenses Do Not Grant Trademark Rights

The software published by CachyOS is available under open-source licenses. These licenses grant you rights to the **code**. They do **not** grant you any right to use the Trademarks.

If you fork, modify, or redistribute software published by CachyOS as allowed by its license, you must remove the CachyOS name, logo, and branding and replace them with your own, so that your version cannot be mistaken for CachyOS. The only exception is the package name of community packages, as described in the [Community Packages](#31-community-packages) section.
You remain free to state truthfully that your work is based on CachyOS, as described in the [Use That Does Not Require Permission](#3-use-that-does-not-require-permission) section.

Nothing in this policy grants any right to redistribute the CachyOS repository or its packages. This remains governed by the [Repository Usage Policy](/policy/repository_policy).

## 6. Logo Usage

Where the use of the CachyOS logo is permitted by this policy or by written permission:

- Use the official logo files without modification.
- Keep the original proportions when scaling and do not distort, rotate, recolor, or add effects to the logo.
- Keep enough clear space around the logo and make sure it is clearly legible on the background it is placed on.
- Do not combine the logo with other logos, text, or graphics, and do not use it as part of your own logo.

## 7. Requesting Permission

If you would like to use the Trademarks in a way that is not permitted by this policy, please contact us at [admin@cachyos.org](mailto:admin@cachyos.org) before you start, and describe what you intend to do.

Permission is only valid if it is given in writing. It may be subject to conditions and can be revoked at any time, in particular if the conditions are no longer met. There is no entitlement to permission.

## 8. Reporting Misuse

If you become aware of any use of the Trademarks that you believe violates this policy, please report it to us at [admin@cachyos.org](mailto:admin@cachyos.org).

We reserve the right to take action against any unauthorized use of the Trademarks.
